Good to know

Questions about EmbroBooks

Is EmbroBooks a different software from SorathERP?
No. EmbroBooks is the embroidery edition of SorathERP, made by the same team at Sorath Software Technologies. It is the same software under a name and plan meant for embroidery businesses.
Why do the web and mobile apps say SorathERP?
The Windows desktop app is branded EmbroBooks. The web and mobile companion apps are the SorathERP apps — you sign in with the same login, see the same data, and they show EmbroBooks once you are signed in.
Can I use it only for sales and purchase billing?
Yes. Billing, accounts and GST work on their own. You can switch the embroidery job work screens on or off for each company in its settings.
Can I keep more than one company?
Yes. One licence covers up to 5 companies and 5 users. Each company keeps its own books.
Can I move to SorathERP Complete later?
Yes. Your data carries over and you do not need to reinstall.
Are there any other charges?
The same as SorathERP Complete. E-way bills and e-invoices are billed per document at our pass-through cost against the GST network, ₹1/document from document 1. Scan-to-upload (Purchase Bill, Purchase Challan or Embroidery Inward Challan) is ₹0.25/scan from the first scan. Extra user or company slots can be added for ₹1,000/yr each. GST verification is always free. See full pricing →
Does the free trial need a credit card?
No. The 30-day trial is the full product with no card and no commitment.
Is migration from Tally or Excel free?
Yes. Our team imports your parties, items, opening balances and pending bills — usually within a day.
What does the annual AMC cover?
Product updates and support for the year — including GST rule changes, new report formats and version upgrades. Your licence itself is one-time and does not expire if you skip an AMC year; you simply stop receiving updates and priority support until you renew.
Are the prices inclusive of GST?
No. ₹9,999 for the first year and ₹5,000 per year AMC are both exclusive of GST; 18% GST is added on your invoice.
